# AMVO roadmap

## v0.1 — implemented foundation

- public-source case registry, schema validation and metadata-only intake;
- deterministic offline exact rational and polynomial coefficient checks;
- SHA-256 artifact integrity with traversal/symlink guards;
- explicit refusal to infer external theorem validity;
- stable machine-readable reports and bilingual static UI;
- regression tests, GitHub Actions CI, public-data screening;
- agent, license, rights, correction and research governance policies.

## v0.2 — robust public literature intake

- commit/DOI pinning, source checksum and rights status;
- explicit theorem/lemma/conjecture/revision/withdrawal record types;
- source-claim dependency and correction timeline;
- publisher/authorship credits separated from reviewer credits.

## v0.3 — genuine formal-checking services

- sandboxed Lean 4 proof checker with pinned mathlib, theorem text, imports and axiom report;
- formal theorem vs natural-language statement matching;
- independent replay receipts and resource-capped execution.

## v0.4 — independent mathematical research verification

- optional Coq/Isabelle and symbolic engines;
- independent proof reconstruction and counterexample exploration;
- reviewer conflicts, audit authority tiers, open correction submissions.

## v1 — observatory at scale

- periodic public research discovery, controlled admission;
- AMRAL public export-only interface, SREG/OCME adapters where licensed;
- provenance API, searchable claim graph;
- editorial projection to research reporting and AI math magazine.

AMVO v0.1 does NOT independently verify any external OpenAI theorem, prove a Millennium problem, run Lean, or access private AMRAL resources.
